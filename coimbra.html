COIMBRA
TREES

10 most common tree species in Coimbra, Portugal — Environment, Comfort and Energy Efficiency. Each tree as an architecture device: shade, wind, water.

Coimbra, Portugal — Choupal / Vale de Canas / Av. Sá da Bandeira — 2026

Choupo-negro

Populus nigra
Mata do Choupal, Mondego
Riparian
Architecture: planted since 1791 to fix banks and diffuse floods; linear forest protecting Baixa / Santa Clara.
Deciduous fast shade in summer, sun in winter. Evapotranspirative cooling.
Fonte: PT Wikipedia Choupal

Plátano

Platanus x hispanica
Av. Sá da Bandeira, Emídio Navarro
Urban
Architecture: avenue tree shading limestone facades, sidewalks and Metrobus stops; >40m in Choupal.
Wide deciduous crown = passive solar control.
Fonte: Polígrafo Coimbra

Freixo

Fraxinus angustifolia
Choupal sub-bosque
Riparian
Architecture: follows valeiros, quintas and Mondego stone walls; filtered shade for courtyards.
Deciduous, tolerates wet soil near foundations.
Fonte: Espaços verdes Concelho Coimbra

Amieiro

Alnus glutinosa
Mata climácica ribeirinha
Riparian
Architecture: stabilizes banks by bridges, cycle paths and river houses.
River microclimate, erosion protection for quays.
Fonte: PT Wikipedia Choupal

Salgueiro-negro

Salix atrocinerea
Mondego / Paul de Arzila
Riparian
Architecture: riverfront parks, very high transpiration = cool island by river buildings.
Traditional pruning keeps views open.
Fonte: Espaços verdes Concelho Coimbra (1library)

Salgueiro-branco

Salix alba
Choupal Zona Central
Riparian
Architecture: larger than atrocinerea, for picnic / sport clearings.
Light shade for lightweight timber structures.
Fonte: Espaços verdes Concelho Coimbra

Lodão

Celtis australis
Choupal >40m, Alta, largos
Urban
Architecture: historic square / cloister tree; low-aggressive roots for limestone paving.
Dense deciduous crown shades heat-storing stone.
Fonte: Flora-On — sebes em torno de Coimbra

Ulmeiro

Ulmus minor
Ruas, adros, conventos
Urban
Architecture: vase shape creates vault over narrow Alta / Baixa streets.
Channels breeze, street canyon shading.
Fonte: PT Wikipedia Choupal

Eucalipto

Eucalyptus globulus / diversicolor
Vale de Canas — 73m, tallest in Europe
Exotic
Architecture: contrast with stone; evergreen permanent shade, high water use.
Negative case: blocks winter solar gain. Energy debate.
Fonte: Florestas.pt Vale de Canas

Ácer / Bordo

Acer spp.
Choupal — now dominant, post-Leslie 5000 trees
Urban / Exotic
Architecture: autumn colour for university patios and schools; medium scale for 2-3 storey buildings.
Deciduous: summer shade, winter permeability.
Fonte: Espaços verdes Concelho Coimbra / ICNF 2021
